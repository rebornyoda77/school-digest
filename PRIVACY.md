# Privacy Policy

**School Digest** is a personal script that reads email from my own Gmail account and emails me a summary. It is used only by its developer, for personal use.

_Last updated: November 16, 2026_

## What the app accesses

- It reads messages in the developer's own Gmail account that come from school-related senders.
- It sends one summary email from that account to that same account.

## How data is used

- Email content is used only to produce the summary.
- To write the summary, the text of those emails is sent to Anthropic's Claude API. Anthropic's terms and privacy policy govern that processing.
- The app does not use email data for advertising, analytics or any other purpose.
